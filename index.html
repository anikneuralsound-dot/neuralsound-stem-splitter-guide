How to separate vocals and instruments from a song

Every finished song is one mixed file, with the voice and every instrument blended together. The AI stem splitter from NeuralSound pulls that mix apart into separate stems, so the vocals and the music each end up on their own.

What "separating a song" means

Music separation, also called stem separation, takes one mixed recording and splits it into separate parts called stems. A vocal stem is just the singing. An instrumental stem is everything except the singing. Once a song is split, you can mute, solo, remix or study any part of it.

It helps singers who want a backing track, musicians learning a part, remixers looking for clean material, and creators who need to tidy up the audio behind a video.

How to split a song, step by step

  1. Open the stem splitterGo to the NeuralSound music separation tool in your browser, or use the NeuralSound app on Android or iOS.
  2. Upload your songChoose the track you want to work on. Start with the best-quality copy you have, because a clean source gives cleaner stems.
  3. Let the AI separate itNeuralSound pulls the mix apart into separate stems, so the vocals and the music are no longer locked together.
  4. Preview, then downloadListen to each part before you save it, so you know you have the one you need. Then download the stems you want to keep.

Tips for cleaner results

  • Use the best source you have. A clean, good-quality file separates better than a compressed or re-recorded copy.
  • Listen on headphones. Small leftovers are easiest to hear in the quiet moments between phrases.
  • Keep your original file. You can always go back and separate it again for a different part.
  • Check the rights. If you publish a cover, remix or video that uses someone else's music, make sure you are allowed to use it.
